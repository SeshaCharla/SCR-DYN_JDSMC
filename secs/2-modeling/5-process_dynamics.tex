\subsection{Complete Process Dynamics \label{sec::complete_proc_dyn}}

From the preceding derivations, the complete discrete process dynamics of the SCR-ASC system are summarized as:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Urea dosing dynamics (equation~\ref{eqn::urea_dosing}):
    \begin{align}
    \con{\ammonia}^{in} (k) &= \nu_u \frac{u_{inj}(k)}{F(k)}
    \end{align}

    \item $\nox$ reduction dynamics (equation~\ref{eqn::NOX_process}):
    \begin{align}
    \con{\nox}^{out} (k + 1) &= \con{\nox}^{in} (k) \lr{1 - k_{s2v} k_{scr} \sigma(k) \tau}
    \end{align}

    \item Ammonia adsorption/desorption dynamics with catalyst saturation:
    \begin{align}
        \sigma^{ub}(k+1) &= \sigma(k) + t_s k_{ads} \con{\ammonia}^{in} (k) \lr{\Gamma - \sigma(k)} \notag \\
                         & \quad - t_s \lr{k_{oxi} + k_{des}} \sigma(k) - t_s k_{scr} \con{\nox}^{in}(k) \sigma(k)
                \label{eqn::sigma_process}\\
        \sigma(k) &= g_{sat} \lr{\sigma^{ub}(k)}  \label{eqn::sigma_sat}
    \end{align}
\end{enumerate}

Note that the tailpipe (gaseous) ammonia dynamics do not affect the $\nox$ reduction dynamics. Furthermore, tailpipe
ammonia measurements are unavailable on production vehicles. Accordingly, the subsequent development focuses on the
$\nox$ reduction input-output model using only the $\nox$ and ammonia adsorption dynamics.
